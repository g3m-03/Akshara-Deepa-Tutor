# 🪔 Akshara-Deepa Tutor

A self-study companion for 10th grade (SSLC) students in rural India.

## 📱 About
Akshara-Deepa turns the SSLC syllabus into an interactive Mission Map. Students can track chapter progress, take quizzes, and visualize their subject mastery — all 100% offline.

## 🚨 Problem Statement
After-school learning in rural areas is unguided. Students have textbooks but no way to track their chapter mastery or identify weak subjects. These gaps only surface during final exams, leading to poor results and dropouts.

## ✨ Features
- **Syllabus Tracker** — Expandable checklist of all chapters across Science, Math, and Social Studies
- **Quiz Mode** — 5 MCQ questions per subject with a 30-second timer and instant answer feedback
- **Strength Map** — Radar chart showing subject-wise mastery (Strong / Improving / Needs Focus)
- **Daily Goal** — Nudges students to complete at least one topic per day

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- Kotlin (Android)
- SharedPreferences (offline storage)
- MPAndroidChart (radar chart)
- Navigation Component
- Min SDK 21 — works on most Android devices

## 📴 Offline First
No internet required. All data is stored locally on the device.

## 🚀 Demo Flow
1. Open app → see dashboard with 0% progress
2. Syllabus → expand Science → tick chapters → progress updates live
3. Quiz → pick subject → answer 5 questions with timer → see score
4. Strength Map → radar chart reflects quiz scores
5. Home → daily goal card turns green

## 🎯 Impact
- Standardizes self-study for rural students
- Introduces data-driven learning early
- Catches learning gaps before final exams
